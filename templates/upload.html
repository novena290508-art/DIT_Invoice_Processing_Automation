Supported Schema & Rules

  • Allowed Formats: .csv (UTF-8, UTF-8 BOM, Latin-1) and .xlsx
  • Required Columns: invoice_number, vendor_name, invoice_date, subtotal, tax_amount, total_amount, currency
  • Validation: Mandatory fields, valid dates, numeric/non-negative amounts, and subtotal + tax_amount = total_amount (±0.01).
  • Zero-Value Invoices: All-zero amounts are flagged as Warning / Review Required.
  • Duplicate Detection: Normalized vendor_name + invoice_number (case- and whitespace-insensitive).
